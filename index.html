How it works

Configure once. Let employees decide.

Three steps. No HRIS replacement. No six-month implementation.

01

Set the structure

You define the parameters — comp categories, RRSP caps, flex pool size, equity vesting terms. The system enforces your rules automatically. You stay in control of the total cost.

02

Employees set their mix

Each employee logs in and moves sliders. Base vs. RRSP vs. equity vs. dental vs. medical. Real-time tax impact updates as they go. They see exactly what their choices mean for take-home pay.

03

One-click approval

You get a notification. Review the election. Click approve. The employee gets a confirmation and PDF summary. No negotiation, no spreadsheet, no back-and-forth. It's done in under a minute.

Common questions.

Does this replace our HRIS?

No. Composable sits alongside your existing HR stack. It handles the comp configuration and approval workflow — your HRIS handles payroll processing and employee records. No migration required.

Is this CRA-compliant?

Yes. The RRSP contribution flow, HSA treatment, and salary deferral mechanics are built to CRA standards from day one. We're not guessing at this — compliance depth is a core part of the product, not an afterthought.

What if an employee makes a choice we wouldn't approve?

You define the constraints. Employees can only configure within the ranges you set. If they submit something outside policy, the system won't allow it. You only see elections that are already within bounds.

How is this different from a benefits administration platform?

Benefits admin tools (Forma, Benepass) handle spending accounts for wellness and perks. Composable handles the comp structure itself — how salary, RRSP, equity, and flex benefits combine from the total employer cost. Different layer entirely.

What does the design partner program involve?

Monthly calls with the founding team. Direct input into the product roadmap. Early access to new features before general release. Preferred pricing locked in before we launch publicly. We work with you, not just for you.
