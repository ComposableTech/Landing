How it works

Three steps. One afternoon.

Set it up before lunch. Your employees configure their own comp before dinner. HR approves from their phone.

01

You set the parameters

Define comp types, floors, caps, and eligibility rules. Base salary minimum, deferred comp ceiling, equity options. Everything within CRA and IRS safe harbours. Takes about 20 minutes.

02

Employees configure their mix

Each employee gets a personalized configurator. They move sliders to set their base/deferred/equity allocation. Real-time tax impact updates as they drag. No HR involvement required.

03

You approve in one click

HR receives a clean summary email. One click to approve or reject. Approved elections generate a PDF confirmation for both parties. Audit trail built in from day one.

Questions

Probably wondering...

Do we need to change our HRIS?
No. Composable sits alongside your existing HRIS — it doesn't replace it. Elected comp structures are documented in Composable and reconciled with your payroll team. HRIS integrations are on the roadmap for post-pilot.
Is this actually CRA compliant?
Yes. We've scoped the comp types and limits to what's compliant under CRA rules — including the deferred compensation limits and eligible arrangements. Every election generates documentation. We recommend legal/tax counsel review before going live.
What if an employee makes a choice they regret?
Elections are locked for the plan year (configurable). You set the change window. HR can override in exceptional circumstances. The system documents every change with timestamps and approver details.
Can we control which employees have access?
Completely. You define eligibility rules — by role, level, salary band, or tenure. Only eligible employees see the configurator. Everyone else sees a standard comp summary.
What does the design partner commitment involve?
A lightweight LOI outlining the engagement terms, a commitment to run at least one election cycle with real employees, and monthly feedback calls with the founding team. No long-term lock-in for the pilot period.
What happens to our data?
Stored in Supabase with row-level security — employees can only see their own data, HR admins see only their company's data. No PII is shared with any third party except Resend for email delivery. SOC 2 in progress.
